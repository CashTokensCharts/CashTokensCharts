Historical data notes

CauldronSwap omits intervals with no trades. The chart leaves these gaps instead of inventing zero-price candles.

The first CauldronSwap pool date is not the token creation date. This prototype has not yet verified the on-chain CashToken genesis timestamp. “First available candle” means the earliest trade candle returned for the selected range, not necessarily the token’s first-ever trade.

History requests start at the CashTokens launch date (May 15, 2023), then rely on the API’s available trade history.
